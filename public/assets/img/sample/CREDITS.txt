Sample photos that come with the site
======================================

These are free stock photos, used so the site looks finished before the owner adds her own. They are not
photos from Otroško animiranje Ema's events. Replace them in the editor (the overview lists each one) with
your own photos, published with the parents' consent.

All were uploaded in 2020-2021 by the photographers below, before photorealistic AI images existed, and
were checked for brand logos, readable text and recognisable adults. Cropped and resized for this site;
all metadata removed. Files ending in -320w / -480w are smaller copies of the photo with the same name,
which the pages offer to small screens (srcset); they are covered by the same credit.

Unsplash License (https://unsplash.com/license): free for commercial use, no attribution required.
Neither licence allows implying that the people shown endorse a business, so these photos are only
illustrations and must not be presented as customers or as photos from real parties.
